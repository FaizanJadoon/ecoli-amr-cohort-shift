Upstream feature extraction and model-training scripts are not complete in this repository, so this package does not claim a fresh end-to-end retraining run.

Scope and scientific position

This is an exploratory retrospective cohort-shift audit in Escherichia coli across ciprofloxacin, ceftriaxone, and gentamicin. Model scores are reported as model outputs; they are not treated as calibrated probabilities unless a specific calibration analysis says so. The frozen test split remains excluded.

Supported findings

  • Antibiotic-specific validation reporting and false-susceptible rates
  • Variation in false-susceptible errors across held-out cohorts
  • Depth-dependent loss of resistance-locus coverage
  • Calibration did not improve reportability under the evaluated policy

Not established

  • Coverage-dependent change in AMR classifier predictions
  • Improvement from a combined coverage- and lineage-aware policy
  • False-susceptible risk control under simultaneous depth and lineage shift
  • Independent untouched-test performance
  • Clinical safety or deployment readiness
